Course Credits

This course combines approximately 120 hours of instruction, laboratory, outside of class work, and assignments for 4 credits.

Prerequisites

A minimum grade of D- is required in ST 422 or ST 522.

Course Description

  • OSU Academic Catalog: Review of probability, including univariate distributions and limit theorems. Random-number generation and simulation of statistical distributions. Bootstrap estimates of standard error. Variance reduction techniques. Emphasis on the use of computation in statistics using the S-Plus or MATLAB programming language.
  • We will use R instead of S-Plus or MATLAB as the major programming language.

Course Content

Monte Carlo methods, variance reduction techniques, optimization algorithms, bootstrapping

Learning Outcomes

  • Students will be able to recognize the statistical theory behind statistical simulation and computation.
  • Students will be able to apply the methods and software to perform statistical simulation and computation.

Homework

  • Homework will be assigned on a regular basis. Unless otherwise noted, these are to be handed in and will be graded.
  • The due dates for the homework assignments can be found under the Assignments section on Canvas.
  • Late homework will not be accepted except in very rare situations where a student has a legitimate reason and has informed the instructor early enough for a special arrangement.
  • ST 541 students will have an extra problem in each homework assignment.

Examination/Project

  • There will be one midterm exam and one final take-home project.
  • No makeup exam or project is allowed except in very rare situations where a student has a legitimate reason and has informed the instructor early enough for a special arrangement.

Grading

  • 40% on the homework, 30% on the midterm exam, and 30% on the final project.
  • Letter grade assignment: A (93-100), A- (90-92), B+ (86-89), B (80-85), B- (73-79), C+ (66-72), C (60-65), C- or lower (0-59).
  • Appropriate curved grading might be used to assign letter grades depending on the performance of the whole class.
  • A student may request that an incomplete (for a course that has not been completed) be granted by an instructor, if the reasons for the incomplete are acceptable to the instructor, and the student is passing the course at the time of the request. Please refer to https://registrar.oregonstate.edu/incomplete-grade-policy for more information.

Generative AI (GenAI)

For this course, you must be the sole author of all work. You may use GenAI in some minor ways. For example, unless otherwise specified in the assignment, you may use GenAI to

  • brainstorm and refine your ideas,
  • find information on your topic,
  • draft an outline to organize your thoughts,
  • check grammar and refine your writing.

You may NOT use GenAI to

  • write a draft of a homework solution,
  • write a draft of R programs,
  • generate entire sentences, paragraphs, or reports,
  • generate entire presentation slides.

Please contact the instructor if you have questions about specific GenAI tools and the allowable usage of GenAI tools in this course so that, together, we can ensure that we are using these tools in productive and ethical ways.
